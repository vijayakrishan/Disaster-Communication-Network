Methodology & Scientific Intent

The model estimates disaster risk using environmental and temporal weather patterns. It is intended as decision support and does not guarantee that a disaster will occur.

Risk scores reflect pattern alignment with historical Tamil Nadu torrential rainfall events rather than calibrated absolute physical probability.
